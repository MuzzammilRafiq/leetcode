The most-asked mediums and hards, shuffled and unlabeled the way an interview serves them. Work top to bottom and try to beat each target time.

    How to use
    • Company-tagged sets run roughly 25% easy, 50% medium, 25% hard; Google leans harder (LeetCode Discuss).
    • A typical onsite round is 1 hard or 2 mediums, and many reported “hards” are tough mediums (Taro, Blind).
    • Indian product companies do ask hards: a 2026 Zepto SDE-1 round opened with IPO (LeetCode Discuss).

    Target times come from each problem’s acceptance rate:

    LevelAcceptanceTarget
    Medium65% and up10–15 min
    Medium40–64%15–25 min
    Mediumunder 40%25–30 min
    Hard55% and up25–35 min
    Hard35–54%35–45 min
    Hardunder 35%45+ min

    When practising, allow about 1.5× the band before reading hints. Acceptance is a rough proxy: low-rate problems like String to Integer are edge-case heavy, not conceptually hard.
